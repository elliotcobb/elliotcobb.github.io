A Poem From Burning Man

So many characters, especially Russians There's so much dust you spend next week brushin Plenty of kids and men with no pants. Too many djs and not enough plants It's easy to flirt and make friends with whoever. By day you'll sweat and by night need a sweater. How lovely to find oneself so surprised by what people construct for such limited time Fire breathing octopus, trippy temple with oculus. Unprompted Invite to upside down pineapple party, didn't lift a finger but got meals that were hearty
